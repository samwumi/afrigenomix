# SEO Checklist for Afrigenomix

## ✅ Immediate Actions (Do Today!)

### 1. Google Search Console Setup
**Priority: CRITICAL**

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://afrigenomix.com`
4. Choose verification method:
   - **Recommended**: HTML file upload
   - Download the verification file
   - Upload it to `/public/` folder
   - Replace the placeholder `google-site-verification.html`
5. Click "Verify"
6. Submit your sitemap: `https://afrigenomix.com/sitemap.xml`

**Time: 10 minutes**

---

### 2. Submit to Google
**Priority: HIGH**

After Search Console verification:
1. In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps"
2. Add sitemap URL: `https://afrigenomix.com/sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"

Google will start crawling your site within 24-48 hours.

**Time: 2 minutes**

---

### 3. Create Google Business Profile
**Priority: HIGH**

1. Go to https://business.google.com
2. Click "Manage now"
3. Enter business details:
   - Name: Afrigenomix
   - Category: Medical Laboratory / DNA Testing Service
   - Location: Your office address in Nigeria
   - Phone: Your business phone
   - Website: https://afrigenomix.com
4. Verify your business (via mail/phone)

**Benefits:** Shows up in Google Maps and local searches

**Time: 15 minutes**

---

### 4. Bing Webmaster Tools
**Priority: MEDIUM**

1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Sign in with Microsoft account
3. Add your site: `https://afrigenomix.com`
4. Verify ownership
5. Submit sitemap: `https://afrigenomix.com/sitemap.xml`

**Time: 5 minutes**

## 🎯 Success Metrics

Track these in Google Search Console:
- **Impressions:** How many times your site shows in search
- **Clicks:** How many people click to visit
- **CTR (Click-through rate):** % of impressions that become clicks
- **Average Position:** Where you rank (aim for position 1-10)

### Goals:
- Month 1: Get indexed (100+ pages)
- Month 2: 100+ clicks/month
- Month 3: 500+ clicks/month
- Month 6: 2,000+ clicks/month

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Keyword stuff (using keywords too many times)
- Copy content from other sites
- Buy backlinks
- Hide text on pages
- Use auto-generated content

✅ **Do:**
- Write naturally for humans
- Create original, valuable content
- Get organic backlinks
- Keep content visible
- Write unique articles

## 📞 Need Help?

If Google hasn't indexed your site after 2 weeks:
1. Check Search Console for errors
2. Verify robots.txt isn't blocking Google
3. Check that sitemap is submitted
4. Request indexing manually in Search Console
