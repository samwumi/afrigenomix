## 🎯 Business Model Compliance

### ✅ Platform Positioning Maintained

**Throughout the application, we consistently emphasize:**
- Afrigenomix is a **coordination platform**
- We **connect customers to laboratories**
- Testing is conducted by **verified laboratory partners**
- We **coordinate** sample collection, not conduct it
- Results come from **laboratory partners**, delivered through our platform

**Specific Messaging Examples:**
- Hero: "Connect with ISO-accredited laboratories"
- How It Works: "We match you with the appropriate verified laboratory partner"
- Lab Network: "Afrigenomix coordinates access—our partners conduct the testing"
- Test Finder Result: "Testing coordinated by Afrigenomix...we'll connect you with our verified laboratory partners"

**Never Claims Afrigenomix:**
- Conducts testing itself
- Is a laboratory
- Performs DNA analysis
- Holds laboratory accreditations (only partners do)
