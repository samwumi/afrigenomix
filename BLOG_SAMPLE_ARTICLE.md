## What is DNA Paternity Testing?

DNA paternity testing is a scientific method used to determine the biological relationship between a father and child. In Africa, access to reliable DNA testing has become increasingly important for:

- Legal proceedings and custody cases
- Immigration applications
- Personal peace of mind
- Medical history documentation

### How DNA Testing Works

The process involves three main steps:

1. **Sample Collection**: Cheek swabs are collected from the alleged father and child
2. **Laboratory Analysis**: DNA markers are analyzed and compared
3. **Results Interpretation**: Statistical probability is calculated

> DNA testing is over 99.99% accurate in confirming paternity and 100% accurate in excluding it.

## Types of DNA Tests Available

### Legal DNA Testing

Legal DNA tests are court-admissible and follow strict chain of custody protocols. They are required for:

- Court cases
- Immigration applications  
- Child support disputes
- Inheritance claims

### Peace of Mind Testing

These tests provide the same accuracy but without legal documentation. Perfect for:

- Personal knowledge
- Family planning decisions
- Relationship clarity

## Important Considerations

Before getting a DNA test, consider:

- **Cost**: Ranges from ₦50,000 to ₦150,000 depending on type
- **Turnaround Time**: Typically 3-5 business days
- **Privacy**: All results are 100% confidential
- **Legal Status**: Ensure you choose legal testing if needed for court

### Common Questions

**Q: Do I need the mother's sample?**  
A: No, paternity can be determined with just father and child samples, though including the mother increases accuracy slightly.

**Q: Can the test be done before birth?**  
A: Yes! Non-invasive prenatal paternity testing is available from 8 weeks of pregnancy.

**Q: How accurate are home DNA test kits?**  
A: While convenient, they're not legally admissible. For legal matters, use accredited laboratories only.
