## 💡 **PRO TIPS:**

### **1. Compelling Titles:**
- ✅ Keep under 60 characters
- ✅ Front-load keywords
- ✅ Make it intriguing
- ❌ Don't use clickbait

**Good:**
- "Ebola Outbreak: Genetic Factors & Public Health Response"
- "Understanding Sickle Cell Disease Through Genetics"

**Bad:**
- "You Won't Believe What Scientists Found..."
- "This One Weird Trick..."

### **2. Engaging Descriptions:**
- ✅ 150-160 characters ideal
- ✅ Include call-to-action
- ✅ Mention key benefit
- ❌ Don't duplicate title

**Good:**
- "Learn how genetic testing can prevent disease outbreaks in Africa. Discover the role of genomics in public health response and preparedness."

**Bad:**
- "Article about Ebola" (too short)
- "In this comprehensive article we will discuss..." (boring)

### **3. Share Strategically:**
- **Best times:**
  - WhatsApp: 7-9 PM (after work)
  - Facebook: 1-4 PM (lunch/afternoon)
  - Twitter: 12-3 PM (midday)
  - LinkedIn: 7-9 AM (morning commute)

## 🔍 **TROUBLESHOOTING:**

### **"Preview not showing on WhatsApp"**
**Causes:**
- Link shared too quickly after publishing
- WhatsApp cached old version
- Network issues

**Fix:**
1. Wait 1-2 minutes after publishing
2. Delete message and re-send link
3. Try on different device
4. Check link works in browser first

### **"Old image showing"**
**Causes:**
- Facebook/WhatsApp cached old version
- Article updated but cache not cleared

**Fix:**
1. Use Facebook Debugger to scrape again
2. Wait 24 hours for cache to expire
3. Share with query parameter: `?v=2` to bypass cache

### **"Image not loading"**
**Causes:**
- Image URL is relative, not absolute
- Image hosted on slow server
- Image too large

**Fix:**
- Already fixed! We ensure absolute URLs
- Unsplash CDN is fast
- Images optimized at 1200x630px
